News Switcher privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers the News Switcher website (rednick.github.io/news-switcher) and the News Switcher app for Android and Android TV.

What News Switcher collects

Nothing. News Switcher has no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no server of its own. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

Settings saved on your device

Your choices, such as which channels to rotate through and whether to show the channel information, are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

YouTube

The news streams are the broadcasters' own live streams, played with YouTube's embedded player. When a stream plays, your device connects to YouTube, and YouTube (Google) may collect information such as your IP address, device details and viewing activity, and may use cookies. That is covered by Google's privacy policy and the YouTube Terms of Service, not by this policy.

Hosting

The website and the page the app displays are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses to run and secure its service, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

News Switcher isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
