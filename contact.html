About News Switcher

News Switcher is a news aggregator. It plays broadcasters' own official 24-hour live news streams, using YouTube's embedded player, and moves to the next channel one minute before every hour. News Switcher doesn't make or edit any news itself. Each stream belongs to its broadcaster, so questions about what a channel shows are best sent to that broadcaster.
